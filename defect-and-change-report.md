This report audits Appendix A as a baseline artifact, records the source-backed semantic changes embodied in the seven-skill portfolio, and maps every change to behavioral coverage. ## Audit verdict and evidence boundary

Appendix A had a sound teaching kernel: identify the proposition, look for important actions hidden in nouns, recover supported actors when agency matters, preserve useful nominalizations, allow information flow to defeat an active-at-all-costs rewrite, and leave clean sentences alone. It was not safe as a portfolio skill because its trigger description claimed adjacent fault classes, several heuristics were phrased as universal rules, passive and agent-suppression decisions were incomplete, its first worked revision invented an actor, and its provenance did not distinguish primary from secondary or full from partial access.

The canonical revision keeps `sentence-clarity` narrow. Cross-sentence old/given-before-new ordering and stress belong to `cohesion-emphasis`; residual padding belongs to `concision`; purpose-driven sentence architecture belongs to `sentence-variety`; document purpose, argument hierarchy, and disputed usage remain separate.

## Residual uncertainty and acceptance impact

1. **Source completeness:** Diátaxis and the Gopen–Swan article were fully inspected; the Williams/Bizup, Lanham, Gopen book, Tufte, Minto, Garner, and White-revised copyrighted works were not. The package is therefore build-ready and artifact-complete, but it cannot honestly satisfy a literal full-corpus-exhaustive acceptance criterion for those books.
2. **Edition relations:** *Basics* fifth and *Lessons* thirteenth are related, not interchangeable. Minto’s 2026 Pearson third edition and her 1996 author-designated expanded text are both recorded; their content-level relation remains unresolved.
3. **Derived rules:** Portfolio routing, pass order, preservation guardrails, intentional mixed-document boundaries, pragmatic MECE limits, and current-corpus protocol are traceable design inferences, not attributed verbatim to a single author.
4. **Empirical status:** Gopen’s structural positions are editorial heuristics. Independent information-structure and dependency-length findings support components, not a universal fixed-reader-expectation model.
5. **Large reference limits:** Garner’s subscription-protected A–Z corpus prevents a complete headword inventory. The supplied index is a verified seed/schema; uninspected stages remain explicitly unverified.
6. **Behavioral validation:** Static structure and declarative fixtures can establish traceability and specified behavior; they cannot guarantee identical activation or revision choices across every host/model/context. The package’s validation results must distinguish static pass, fixture specification, and any live-model execution.

No residual uncertainty licenses filling an access gap, inventing a page number or LCI stage, deleting necessary qualification, or overriding the minimal-intervention rule.
