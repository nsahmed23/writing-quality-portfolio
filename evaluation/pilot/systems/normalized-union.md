# Normalized union, detect-only

This candidate combines source-grounded mechanisms into one contextual diagnostic. It is an original synthesis, not an assertion that repeated inherited rules are independent evidence.

## Order

1. Identify the artifact type, audience, purpose, and declared author profile.
2. Mark exact protected regions, including code, frontmatter, table syntax, link targets, quotations, paths, identifiers, and source-bound factual text.
3. Record material propositions: actor, action, quantity, date, attribution, modality, negation, condition, comparison baseline, and causal status.
4. Diagnose evidence and factual-integrity faults.
5. Diagnose structure, cohesion, sentence clarity, concision, syntax, usage, rhythm, formatting, and chatbot residue.
6. Test each candidate finding against contextual KEEP controls and author voice.
7. Return only exact-span findings using the common envelope.

## CHANGE standard

A CHANGE finding must name a concrete reader-facing problem, explain why it is a problem here, and propose the smallest safe operation. Generic AI-likeness, word presence, punctuation presence, sentence length, or statistical style scores do not justify a finding by themselves.

Priority problems include unsupported specificity, vague or missing attribution, evidence mismatch, missing comparison or time scope, causal overclaim, unjustified certainty, ambiguous negation or actor, inconsistent facts across surfaces, vague reference, false agency, overgeneralization, terminology drift, generic filler, inflated importance, redundant summary, formulaic contrast, mechanical fragmentation or cadence, obscuring nominalization, ambiguous attachment, responsibility-hiding passive voice, tone mismatch, chatbot residue, and broken structured content.

## KEEP standard

Preserve a passive when its actor is unknown, irrelevant, already established, or properly backgrounded. Preserve an em dash when it performs real syntactic or voice work. Preserve a triad when all three items are necessary. Preserve technical terminology when it is the precise domain term. Preserve deliberate repetition when it maintains cohesion or emphasis. Preserve purposeful fragments in headings, interface text, notes, or a declared personal voice. Preserve clear second-language phrasing. Preserve justified modality and source-bound attribution.

Never normalize a construction solely because it is commonly labeled AI-like. Never invent a personal detail, actor, number, date, quotation, study, example, or emotional reaction to make prose feel human.

## Suggested operations

Prefer one of: `preserve`, `delete_filler`, `name_actor`, `name_source`, `qualify_claim`, `restore_modality`, `clarify_negation`, `add_baseline`, `add_time_scope`, `align_with_evidence`, `use_consistent_term`, `resolve_reference`, `split_sentence`, `combine_fragments`, `move_modifier`, `activate_verb`, `remove_redundancy`, `remove_chatbot_artifact`, `repair_formatting`, or `request_source`.

If a safe local repair would require information not present in the text or task context, use `request_source` and leave `replacement` null.
