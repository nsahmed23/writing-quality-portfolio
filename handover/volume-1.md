## 3. Project Definition

**Project name.** No official name recorded. Working names in artifacts: "Enterprise Laptop Qualification Program" (the workload) and "Windows Endpoint IaC Program" (the platform). The platform is the program; qualification is one workload on it (DEC, Volume 2).

**Problem being solved.** Three linked problems. First, laptop purchasing decisions lack rigorous, reproducible evidence and leadership-readable justification. Second, Windows endpoint configuration lives only inside the Intune management console, unversioned, with no drift visibility and no pull-request-style change control. Third, compliance posture is proven by manual evidence assembly rather than generated continuously from fleet state.

**Background.** The user previously ran an analytics capstone called DEX (Digital Employee Experience) at the same company: a survey-derived experience score for call-center employees validated against customer-satisfaction data, plus telemetry friction findings from a product called SysTrack. That project ended around late July 2026 under a different sponsor. Its materials sit in this workspace for historical understanding only. Two durable inheritances are allowed: its evidence-governance epistemology (pre-registration, provenance, honest negatives) and its hard-won knowledge of telemetry-source limits. Its branding, figures, and sponsor are not stakeholders or framing for the current work.

**Intended beneficiaries.** The new-hardware team and its leadership line (approve spend with evidence), employees in defined personas (get hardware matched to measured workload), procurement (a locked, enforceable purchase specification), and auditors (pipeline-generated evidence, future state).

**Desired end state.** Five platform phases: (1) Intune state as code, read path: Microsoft Graph snapshots of all Intune configuration into git, giving drift visibility; (2) write path: git is the source of truth, pipelines apply changes, CI gates enforce rules; (3) deployment rings and update waves as code, keyed to device model and persona; (4) compliance as code: security baselines as versioned rules, fleet state from Graph and SysTrack landing in Microsoft Fabric, evaluated continuously, audit evidence generated; (5) closed loop: detected drift triggers remediation, re-verifies, writes evidence automatically. The qualification workload runs on this platform and produces, per decision, a one-page persona-first buying recommendation whose predictions the rollout later verifies.

**Success criteria.** Leadership reads a recommendation in under two minutes and every number traces to an evidence record; drift between intended and actual Intune state is visible; update waves promote on telemetry gates, not installation success alone; recommendations accumulate a measured hit rate (if the ledger is adopted).

**Failure criteria.** Verdicts issued on missing evidence; evidence joined across unequal baselines; the program presented as a DEX deliverable; documents leadership cannot absorb quickly.

**In scope.** Enterprise Windows laptops and their Intune management; the G2a candidate evaluation; persona routing including a virtual-desktop branch; the document and decision system.

**Out of scope.** macOS and iPadOS experience scoring (ruled unmeasurable or uncitable, see FACT-040 and FACT-041); any DEX-branded deliverable; Ansible for production management (lab post-provisioning only).
