# Current evidence protocol

1. Normalize the exact construction and intended sense.
2. Retrieve the actual Garner fifth-edition entry/page; never reconstruct it from snippets.
3. Record audience, formality, English variety, field, medium, and risk tolerance.
4. Check a current dictionary’s entry plus usage labels/paragraph.
5. Query a balanced or edited corpus and a recent corpus; inspect concordance contexts, not counts alone.
6. Separate speech from edited writing and compare regions when relevant.
7. Control homographs, senses, quotation/duplicate material, OCR, and denominators.
8. Consult scholarly grammar or field guidance for syntactic/socially contested issues.
9. Record corpus/version/query/filters/retrieval date and contrary results.
10. Report Garner 5e (2022) separately from current evidence.

Evidence sources and limitations:

- [COCA](https://www.english-corpora.org/coca/): genre-balanced American English; coverage ends before 2026, so not a live monitor.
- [NOW](https://www.english-corpora.org/now/): continuously updated web news; useful for change, biased toward news/web publication.
- [GloWbE](https://www.english-corpora.org/glowbe/): regional World English comparisons; web genre/duplication cautions.
- [iWeb](https://www.english-corpora.org/iweb/): very large web corpus; noisy and not edited-standard by default.
- [BNC2014](https://corpora.lancs.ac.uk/bnc2014/): British evidence; spoken/written components must be distinguished.
- [Google Books Ngram](https://books.google.com/ngrams/info): diachronic print frequency, not context or acceptance; OCR and corpus composition can shift charts.
- [Merriam-Webster usage labels](https://www.merriam-webster.com/help/explanatory-notes/dict-usage): label/history/advice; dictionary inclusion is documentation, not endorsement.

## Cutoff-dated sample checks

Retrieved 2026-08-25; sources were public as of the 2026-08-19 cutoff unless noted.

- **Singular *they*:** Merriam-Webster records singular indefinite and nonbinary senses and plural-form agreement; status depends on antecedent and house policy, not grammatical number alone. [Entry](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/they)
- **Singular/plural *data*:** current Merriam-Webster guidance treats singular mass *data* as normal in most and computing contexts, while plural remains common in scientific/technical writing. [Usage note](https://www.merriam-webster.com/grammar/some-data-about-datum)
- **Restrictive *which*:** current Merriam-Webster guidance permits *which* or *that* in restrictive clauses, with *which* required for nonrestrictive clauses; this rebuts presenting *that-only* as grammar. [Usage note](https://www.merriam-webster.com/grammar/when-to-use-that-and-which)
- ***Irregardless*:** attested and dictionary-entered but labeled nonstandard/widely disliked; *regardless* avoids criticism in formal edited prose. [Entry](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/irregardless)

These are dictionary demonstrations, not a complete Garner inventory or corpus-count study.
