# Searchable usage taxonomy

This is a derived portfolio taxonomy, not a Garner-labeled table:

1. Grammar and syntax
2. Morphology and inflection
3. Meaning, diction, and confusables
4. Idiom, collocation, and preposition choice
5. Spelling, capitalization, and hyphenation
6. Punctuation and typography
7. Pronunciation and stress
8. Register and formality
9. Regional, dialectal, and World English variation
10. Field-specific/legal usage and jargon
11. Bias, dated/offensive language, and inclusive alternatives
12. Redundancy, pleonasm, and verbosity
13. Innovations, skunked terms, disputes, and folklore rules
14. Archaism, euphemism, and hypercorrection

Each issue row stores exact construction/sense, authority/date, LCI only when verified, register, geography, field, variants, exceptions, page/location, current evidence, recommendation, disagreement, and confidence.
