# Exceptions and preservation cases

- **Established topic:** “Authentication occurs before authorization.” *Authentication* and *authorization* are deliberate technical concepts; no actor is missing.
- **Result noun:** “The decision ended the pilot.” The noun names the result under discussion.
- **Appropriate passive:** “The token is signed with the tenant key.” The operation/result matters; the signer may be system-internal or already known.
- **Unknown agent:** “Three records were deleted overnight.” Do not invent *an administrator*.
- **Accountability-sensitive passive:** “The approval was granted without review.” If the actor is known and consequential, recommend naming it; otherwise flag the evidentiary gap rather than guessing.
- **Stative clause:** “The service is unavailable in Region A.” *Is* carries the state; it is not empty.
- **Deliberate periodic form:** “After the final replica catches up and the health check passes, the controller promotes the release.” The conditions legitimately precede the action.
- **Legal/scientific hedge:** Preserve *may*, *typically*, *subject to*, confidence intervals, and attribution unless evidence makes them redundant.
