# Original examples

| Context | Before | Revision / decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional | “A determination of eligibility will be made by the review team.” | “The review team will determine eligibility.” | Known actor and key action were obscured. |
| API | “The creation of a token occurs after validation of the signature.” | “The API validates the signature, then creates a token.” | Supported sequence and actor become visible. |
| Security | “An exploitation of the flaw was carried out.” | “The incident record does not identify who exploited the flaw.” | Do not invent an agent; expose the evidence limit. |
| Architecture | “The replication of events is performed by the broker.” | “The broker replicates events.” | Buried action and light verb. |
| Executive memo | “There was a failure by Finance to provide approval.” | “Finance did not approve the request.” | Removes shell clause while preserving negation. |
| Research | “An association was observed between exposure and response.” | Keep. | Conventional cautious reporting; observer may be irrelevant. |
| Email | “It is my request that you provide a response by Friday.” | “Please respond by Friday.” | Direct request; courtesy retained. |
| Clean technical prose | “Authentication precedes authorization.” | Keep. | Technical nouns are exact topics; no hidden-action problem. |
| Passive-for-flow | “The gateway validates the request. It is then routed to the nearest healthy shard.” | Keep passive in sentence 2. | *It* maintains the request as topic. |
| Long but clear | “If both signatures validate, the gateway forwards the request to the regional queue selected by the routing policy.” | Keep. | Conditions and dependency are recoverable. |
