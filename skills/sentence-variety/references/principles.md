| ID | Rule | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| TUF-ORG-01 | Analyze syntax as a repertoire of choices that produces style | Publisher characterization and visible 14-chapter organization; full book unavailable |
| TUF-SHORT-01 | Use short form for a rhetorical job, not as a readability talisman | Derived from chapter “Short Sentences” |
| TUF-BR-01–04 | Control left-, mid-, and right-branching material around a recoverable kernel | Chapter “Free Modifiers: Branching Sentences”; subcategory detail partly derived |

| TUF-SYM-01 | Let form enact meaning only when it remains clear and apt | Chapter “Syntactic Symbolism”; high-judgment, never default |

The operational fault is form-purpose misalignment, not a repeated word count or length band.
