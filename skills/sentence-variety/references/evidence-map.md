# Evidence map and access limit

Virginia Tufte, *Artful Sentences: Syntax as Style* (Graphics Press, 2006), ISBN 9780961392185. The [publisher announcement](https://www.edwardtufte.com/notebook/artful-sentences-syntax-as-style-by-virginia-tufte-now-published/) and [Internet Archive catalog](https://archive.org/details/artfulsentencess0000tuft) establish the edition and all 14 chapter titles; the book is access-restricted and was not inspected in full.

Visible chapters: Short Sentences; Noun Phrases; Verb Phrases; Adjectives and Adverbs; Prepositions; Conjunctions and Coordination; Dependent Clauses; Sentence Openers and Inversion; Free Modifiers: Branching Sentences; The Appositive; Interrogative, Imperative, Exclamatory; Parallelism; Cohesion; Syntactic Symbolism.

Catalog rows explicitly mark whether provenance is a visible Tufte chapter or portfolio/descriptive-grammar elaboration. The package does **not** claim that Tufte uses every derived label (for example, *polysyndeton* or *periodic sentence*) or gives the stated definition. The [USC obituary](https://dornsife.usc.edu/news/stories/in-memoriam-virginia-tufte/) and Edward Tufte’s own announcement confirm Virginia Tufte was Edward Rolf Tufte’s mother, not wife.
