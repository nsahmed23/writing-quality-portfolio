# Passage-level examples

## Monotonous SVO sequence

Before: “The team tested the gateway. The team found a leak. The team disabled the route. The team opened an incident.”

After: “When testing exposed a leak, the team disabled the route and opened an incident.”

Purpose: subordination encodes cause/time; coordination gives the two responses equal weight.

## Deliberate repetition retained

“No customer data left the region. No backup crossed the boundary. No exception bypassed the policy.” Repetition makes three assurance claims comparable; keep it.

## Choppiness

“The key expired. Requests failed. The queue grew.” If causally related: “After the key expired, requests failed and the queue grew.” If independent findings, retain separate units.

## Sprawl

Split only at genuine rhetorical boundaries; do not shorten a controlled long sentence merely to alter a length profile.
