# Evidence map

Williams/Bizup: *Style: The Basics of Clarity and Grace*, 5th ed., Lesson 7 “Concision” and Lessons 8–10 in the [official TOC](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/style-the-basics-of-clarity-and-grace/P200000002141/9780134109749). Full fifth-edition chapters were unavailable; no fifth-edition page claims are made.

Lanham: *Revising Prose*, 5th ed., Pearson Longman, published 10 July 2006, ©2007, ISBN 9780321441690. Official chapters are listed by [Pearson](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/revising-prose/P200000002251/9780321441690). The eight-step method and page spans come from Maria da Graça L. Castro Pinto’s [peer-reviewed chapter-by-chapter review](https://ojs.letras.up.pt/index.php/LinguarumArena/article/download/3986/3734/11783). The book itself was not fully accessible.

The [Purdue OWL Paramedic Method](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/paramedic_method.html) is secondary/adapted provenance. “Good fat” and preservation rules are evidence-grounded portfolio qualifications, not a claim that every listed exception appears verbatim in Lanham.
