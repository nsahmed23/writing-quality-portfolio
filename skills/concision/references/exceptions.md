# Do-not-cut inventory

- Scientific/evidentiary hedges that encode confidence or scope.
- Legal conditions, exceptions, definitions, and required notices.
- Security warnings, repeated safety constraints, and recovery checks.
- Technical terms whose longer form is more precise than a plain-language substitute.
- Navigation that tells a reader what a section does.
- Courtesy that manages a request or refusal.
- Attribution and citations.
- Repetition that maintains a topic, contrast, parallel series, or learning cue.
- Prepositions required to state a relationship.
- Forms of *be* that express identity, condition, location, or existence.
- Passive clauses that preserve topic flow or responsibly omit an unknown/irrelevant agent.
