# Guarded Paramedic Method

Lanham fifth edition’s eight numbered operations, as reproduced in a peer-reviewed review, are:

1. Circle prepositions.
2. Circle forms of *is*.
3. Find the action: who is doing what to whom?
4. Put the action in a simple active verb.
5. Start fast; remove slow windups.
6. Read aloud with emphasis and feeling.
7. Isolate sentences and mark rhythmic units with `/`.
8. Mark sentence lengths through the passage with `/`.

This portfolio adds a preservation gate before step 1 and a semantic audit after step 8. It treats active voice as a candidate, not a command: flow, agent relevance, register, and evidence may favor passive.

Lard factor: `(original word count − revised word count) / original word count × 100`. Report it descriptively; no percentage is a quality target.

Purdue OWL’s seven-step version is an adaptation, not the complete fifth-edition ordering.
